Technical and organisational measures (art. 32), as built:

- Row-level security on every clinical table; queries run as the signed-in user's role (`src/clinical/db`).
- Staff (doctors, pharmacies, admins) need 2FA (aal2), enforced by the app and by the database.
- Admins see operations, never answers, photos or messages; pharmacies see a narrow view without the condition.
- Every read and write of health data is audited (who, when, which record, never content).
- Clinical pages: no analytics, no third-party scripts, nonce CSP, noindex, out of the sitemap.
- No health data in URLs, logs, analytics, local storage, non-auth cookies, Stripe metadata or e-mail bodies (lint + `tests/unit/health-data-scan.test.ts`).
- Hosting in the EU (Supabase Frankfurt, Vercel functions `fra1`); TLS everywhere; encryption at rest by Supabase.
